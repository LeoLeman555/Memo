The project

MEMO allows a responsible person to program audio reminders through a mobile application and send them to an ESP32 based autonomous device created to aid individuals with intellectual impairments in their time management and daily routines.

Discover our solution

The issue to solve

Elia is a 19 year old woman who suffers from hemiplegia, which causes cognitive issues, and therefore complicates the management of her schedule as well as her daily autonomy, for her as much as for her occupational therapists.

The main objective of MEMO is to provide her with a simple and reliable technological support, to add to her quality of life, while adapting itself to her needs and conserving ease of use.

Inclusive and universal

MEMO is entirely open source so that anyone can recreate Memo, adapt it to individual needs, and help people with disabilities become more independent in their daily lives. All the technical details as well as our history and work process are available on our website.

Application & architecture

The MEMO system’s architecture is based around a smartphone application and embedded firmware.

The mobile application, developed in React Native, allows the user to create, schedule, and send personalised voice messages. The messages are transformed into audio through a Text To Speech service then sent via Bluetooth to the ESP32 microcontroller inside MEMO.

The MicroPython firmware on the ESP32 controls file reception, local storage, the real time clock, and triggering the reminders.

3D design

The enclosure for MEMO was conceived thanks to the Computer Aided Design software Onshape, and designed to favor ease of use as well as transportability.

It was also designed to be robust and easy to handle, as well as integrating bright colours, to help people with visual or cognitive impairments to recognise it.
